CalorieLogger

A calorie and nutrition tracker that keeps your diary on your phone.

Contact support

Questions, bugs or feature ideas: email markdaxisa@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

What it does

  • Log food by search, barcode scan, photo, voice or quick-add calories.
  • Track calories, macros and micronutrients against your own targets.
  • Log your weight and see weekly trends.
  • Optional AI estimates from a photo, a description or a restaurant menu, using an AI service you choose.
  • Optional Apple Health / Health Connect, iCloud or Google Drive sync, reminders and home-screen widgets.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. There is no CalorieLogger account and no CalorieLogger server. Data only leaves your phone when you look up a food online, use an AI feature, or turn on sync. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Go to Settings → Backup & sync. Turn on iCloud (iPhone) or Google Drive (iPhone and Android) sync, or use Export backup to save a file. On the new phone, turn on the same sync or use Restore from file.

How do I set up the AI features?

Go to Settings → AI assistant and choose a provider. You can sign in with a ChatGPT plan or paste an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI or Google Gemini, or point it at any OpenAI-compatible service. Your key stays in your phone's secure storage. Any charges come from your provider, not from CalorieLogger.

Why are AI calorie estimates sometimes off?

AI estimates from photos and descriptions are approximate. Check portion sizes before saving, and edit the entry if needed. Barcode and database foods are usually more accurate.

How do I connect or disconnect Apple Health / Health Connect?

Go to Settings → Health to choose what CalorieLogger reads and saves. To revoke access completely, use the Health app (iPhone: Profile → Apps → CalorieLogger) or Health Connect settings (Android).

A barcode wasn't found. What now?

Barcode data comes from Open Food Facts, a free community database. If a product is missing, create it in CalorieLogger from the nutrition label (a photo of the label works with AI turned on). You can also add it at openfoodfacts.org to help others.

Why do I see ads, and can I change ad tracking?

Ads keep CalorieLogger free. They appear only on the Diary and Week screens, never while you log. On iPhone, you can change tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where it applies (for example in the EU and UK), Settings → Ad privacy choices lets you change your consent. Your diary and health data are never shared with advertisers.

Reminders aren't showing up.

Make sure notifications are allowed for CalorieLogger in your phone's settings, then turn reminders off and on again in Settings → Reminders.

How do I delete my data?

Delete entries in the app, or uninstall CalorieLogger to remove everything on the phone. If you used sync, also remove the backup: on iPhone, go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage. For Google Drive, go to drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps → CalorieLogger → Delete hidden app data. CalorieLogger holds no copy, so nothing else needs to be deleted.

Is CalorieLogger medical advice?

No. CalorieLogger is for personal tracking only. Talk to a health professional before making big changes to your diet, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or have a history of disordered eating.
