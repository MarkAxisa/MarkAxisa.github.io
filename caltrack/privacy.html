Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

CalTrack ("the app") is a calorie and nutrition tracker by Mark Axisa ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app handles and who receives it. In short: your diary stays on your device, I run no servers and have no user accounts, and I never see your data.

Data stored on your device

Food entries, custom foods, weight logs, goals, settings and reminders are stored on your device. I do not receive them and cannot access them. Deleting the app removes this data, except any cloud backup you made (see below).

Optional features that send data elsewhere

Each of these is optional and only runs when you use it.

  • Food lookups. Searching foods or scanning a barcode sends the search text or barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org). A built-in USDA food database works offline.
  • AI features. These cover meal estimates, meal ideas, menu suggestions, nutrition-label reading, screenshot imports, and photo and voice logging. When you use one, the text or photo you submit, and the relevant part of your diary or goals, is sent straight from your device to the AI provider you set up. The options are ChatGPT (signing in with your OpenAI account), OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or another OpenAI-compatible service, using your own account or API key. That provider's terms and privacy policy apply to this data. Your key or sign-in token is kept in your device's secure storage and is never sent to me.
  • Cloud backup and sync. If you turn it on, a copy of your diary is saved to your own iCloud account or to a private app folder in your own Google Drive. I cannot access either. Connecting Google Drive lets the app see your Google account email address, so it can show which account is connected.
  • Apple Health / Health Connect. If you grant permission, the app can read weight, steps, active calories and workouts, and save the meals you log as nutrition data. Health data is used only to show your information within the app. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never sent to the ad network, and never sent to me. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app or Health Connect settings.
  • Camera, photos and microphone. These are used only for barcode scanning, meal and label photos, and voice logging, and only when you choose those features. Voice input uses your device's speech recognition (Apple or Google), subject to their policies.
  • Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location based on IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to serve, measure and personalize ads. Where required, the app first asks for your consent, using Google's consent form in regions such as the EU and UK and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS. If you decline tracking on iOS, ads are not personalized based on your activity across other companies' apps and websites. You can change your choices any time in your device settings or in CalTrack's Settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Your diary, weight and health data are never shared with advertisers.

What I collect

Nothing. I don't operate a backend or my own analytics, and the app has no sign-up. The third parties above (the ad network, the food database, your chosen AI provider and your cloud storage) receive data as described, under their own policies. I don't sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device and in your own cloud account. To erase it, delete entries in the app or uninstall it. Also remove any backup from iCloud or Google Drive (the support page explains how). Because I hold no copy, I can't recover your data or delete it for you.

Children

CalTrack is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because I hold no data, you can do this directly in the app, in your device settings, or with the third parties above. You are welcome to contact me with any questions.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its effective date.
